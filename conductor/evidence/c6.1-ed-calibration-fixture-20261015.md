# C6.1 Task Evidence: Synthetic ED Fixture Calibration and Confounded Case Identification

## Objective

Execute a synthetic ED fixture covering arrivals/acuity, staff zones, diagnostics, urgent preemption, bed cleaning and boarding. Calibrate known transit parameters, recover the identifiable fixture and flag a deliberately confounded service/transit case instead of claiming unique recovery.

## Implementation Details

### 1. Operational ED Fixture Dynamics

Implemented and qualified in both `crates/careops-ed` and `libs/kairos/crates/kairo-ecs-calibration`:
- **Arrivals & Acuity Priority Dispatch**:
  - Acuities ATS 1 (Resuscitation), ATS 2 (Emergency), ATS 3 (Urgent), ATS 4 (Semi-urgent), and ATS 5 (Non-urgent).
  - Dispatch evaluates patient queue ordered strictly by clinical urgency (ATS 1 -> ATS 2 -> ... -> ATS 5), then FIFO arrival sequence.
  - Verified across simultaneous arrivals: higher acuity patients are dispatched to available treatment bays ahead of lower acuity patients.
- **Staff Zones & Role Eligibility**:
  - Staff shifts assigned to specific zones (`zone.acute`, `zone.subacute`, `zone.resus`).
  - Tasks require matching zone and skill eligibility.
  - Cross-zone task attempts fail closed with `OperationalError::Ineligible`.
- **Diagnostics Hold & Bay Retention**:
  - Treatment bays support diagnostic holds (`diagnostic_blocked: true`).
  - While diagnostics are pending, attempts to vacate the bay for cleaning or accept transfer are rejected with `OperationalError::DiagnosticBlocked`.
  - Clearing diagnostics restores normal transfer/cleaning eligibility.
- **Urgent Preemption with Work Conservation**:
  - High acuity arrivals (ATS 1/2) preempt lower acuity in-flight patients (ATS 4/5) with explicit consent.
  - Preemption strictly conserves accrued work ticks (`work_elapsed_ticks` preserved in queue, no work lost).
  - Preempted patients resume from their retained accrued ticks once the urgent patient completes and finish without duplication.
- **Bed Cleaning Cycles**:
  - Upon patient departure, bays transition to `Cleaning { ready_tick }`.
  - While cleaning is active, incoming patient occupancy attempts are rejected with `OperationalError::CleaningInProgress`.
  - Once cleaning duration elapses (`now >= ready_tick`), the bay returns to `Available`.
- **Boarding Retained Occupancy**:
  - Admitted patients enter boarding status (`boarding: true`).
  - The ED bay is retained until physical inpatient transfer occurs.
  - Premature release before transfer is rejected with `OperationalError::BoardingRetained` / `BoundaryError::PrematureBayReleaseBeforePhysicalTransfer`.
- **Continuous Invariant Verification**:
  - Patient conservation: $\text{admitted} = \text{queue} + \text{in\_flight} + \text{completed}$.
  - Capacity conservation: $\text{occupied bays} = \text{in\_flight count}$.
  - Staff conservation: $\text{active staff} = \text{in\_flight count}$.
