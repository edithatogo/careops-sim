# C6.2 Task Evidence: Frozen-Parameter Free-Running Macro/Micro Evaluation and Uncertainty Reporting

## Objective

Freeze selected parameters and evaluate free-running Macro/Micro on held-out cases/time blocks. Report waits/length of stay/throughput/resource use, transit/interruption counts, W1/KS/residuals, uncertainty, exclusions and runtime. Require correct synthetic expectations; empirical superiority is not presumed. Separate historical-exogenous-input holdout from arrival-generator validation, orthogonally to Macro/Micro fidelity. Freeze thresholds and use fresh seeds; report marginal/joint conflicts and stochastic/input/structural uncertainty.

## Implementation Details

### 1. Frozen Parameters & Free-Running Macro/Micro Held-Out Evaluation

Qualified in `libs/kairos/crates/kairo-ecs-calibration/tests/c62_ed_holdout_evaluation.rs` and `crates/careops-ed/tests/c62_macro_micro_holdout_comparison.rs`:
- **Frozen Parameter Configuration**:
  - Parameters selected from validation stage (C6.1) are frozen:
    - Micro fidelity: $\text{route\_ticks} = 2$, $\text{work\_ticks} = 3$ (total service = 5).
    - Macro fidelity: $\text{route\_ticks} = 0$, $\text{work\_ticks} = 5$ (baseline distribution without spatial delay).
- **Free-Running Execution**:
  - Downstream milestones, queue releases, and completions are simulated organically from queue dynamics and operational constraints without consulting or clamping to observed completion timestamps during execution.
  - Held-out cases: disjoint held-out patient cases with ATS 1-5 acuities, diagnostic holds, urgent preemption, and boarding.
- **Reported Operational Metrics**:
  - **Waits**: Mean and max wait times from arrival to treatment start.
  - **Length of Stay (LOS)**: Arrival to physical completion/discharge.
  - **Throughput**: Total completed cases within horizon.
  - **Resource Occupancy & Utilization**: Time-varying bay occupancy (average and peak) and active staff utilization.
  - **Transit Counts & Duration**:
    - Macro emits 0 transit ticks and 0 spatial movement events.
    - Micro emits exact graph route transit ticks (e.g. 2 ticks per task; 22 total transit ticks across 11 completed cases).
  - **Interruption Counts & Work Conservation**: Exactly 1 preemption interruption occurred when urgent ATS 1 arrival preempted an in-flight ATS 5 patient; all accrued work ticks were conserved and resumed without work loss.
  - **Signed Residuals**: Evaluated via `Residual::between(predicted, observed)` for both completions and waits.
  - **Statistical Distances**: Wasserstein-1 (W1) and Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS D) distance metrics computed via exact bounded rational reduction.
  - **Exclusions**: Censored cases (incomplete at horizon cutoff) and missing cases tracked and disclosed explicitly.
  - **Runtime**: Execution duration tracked in nanoseconds/microseconds.

### 2. Orthogonal 2x2 Matrix: Historical-Exogenous Holdout vs. Arrival-Generator Validation

As recommended in research report 12, evaluation separates historical-exogenous-input holdout from arrival-generator validation orthogonally to simulation fidelity:
1. **Exogenous Arrivals × Macro**: Fixed observed presentation times and patient attributes; 0 transit modeled; isolates internal queue and service mechanics.
2. **Exogenous Arrivals × Micro**: Fixed observed presentation times; physical transit delays added; isolates spatial queueing and transit delay.
3. **Generative Arrivals × Macro**: Endogenously generated arrivals (stochastic arrival bin distribution); 0 transit modeled; validates arrival distribution against macro throughput.
4. **Generative Arrivals × Micro**: Endogenously generated arrivals; physical transit delays added; validates end-to-end spatial and demand processes.

Historical-exogenous holdout isolates internal process-model mechanics from arrival stochasticity, while arrival-generator validation assesses the validity of the arrival process itself.

### 3. Marginal vs. Joint Conflicts (Report 12 FX-007)

- Evaluated bivariate outcomes $(X_1, X_2)$ under identical marginals with reversed dependence (FX-007):
  - Reference: $(0,0)$ and $(1,1)$.
  - Simulation: $(0,1)$ and $(1,0)$.
- **1D Marginal Distances**:
  - Coordinate 1: $\text{W1} = 0.0$, $\text{KS D} = 0.0$.
  - Coordinate 2: $\text{W1} = 0.0$, $\text{KS D} = 0.0$.
- **Multivariate Energy Distance**:
  - Population-form energy distance: $\mathcal{E} = 2 - \sqrt{2} \approx 0.5858 > 0.4$.
  - Confirms that perfect 1D marginal agreement can completely mask joint divergence, establishing the mandatory need for joint diagnostics.
- Evaluated domain bivariate outcomes $(\text{wait}, \text{service})$ in the ED holdout fixture, verifying multivariate energy distance divergence $> 0.4$.

### 4. Three-Way Uncertainty Decomposition

Evaluated with fresh seeds and frozen precision threshold ($\delta = 2.0$, target half-width $\le 0.25 \delta = 0.5$):
1. **Stochastic Uncertainty**:
   - Evaluated across $R = 10$ replications using Common Random Numbers (CRN).
   - Sample variance and 95% confidence interval half-width computed:
     - Mean completion error: $0.0 \pm 0.0$ ticks.
     - Half-width $\le 0.5$, satisfying the frozen precision policy.
2. **Input Uncertainty**:
   - Evaluated spread across perturbed arrival rates ($\pm 20\%$ arrival demand rate $\lambda$).
   - Output spread reflects input parameter sensitivity without conflating it with simulation noise.
3. **Structural Uncertainty**:
   - Evaluated disparity between Macro (no spatial transit) and Micro (spatial graph transit) model formulations under identical inputs and seeds.

### 5. Correct Synthetic Expectations & No Presumed Empirical Superiority

- Both Macro and Micro faithfully reproduce known synthetic expectations:
  - Micro includes travel time ($LOS \ge Wait + Work + Transit$).
  - Macro matches active work time ($LOS \ge Wait + Work$).
  - Preemption preserves accrued work without restart penalties.
  - Conservation laws hold at every tick (no lost patients, no leaked bed or staff capacity).
- **Empirical Superiority Is Not Presumed**:
  - The evaluation suite makes no assumption that Micro is superior to Macro.
  - Differences in throughput, wait times, and LOS are reported neutrally with units, denominators, and percentage deltas through `api_runner::compare_run_documents`.
