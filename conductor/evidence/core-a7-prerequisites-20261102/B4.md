team and space intervals in one process. It has no shared-resource wait queue,
upstream atomic-grant guarantee or durable checkpoint. New admissions are at the
known duty start; earlier room waiting reservations are outside this helper.

validated against the supplied room ledger's matching lifecycle history.

early. Escort chains retain the same worker through their declared commitment,
with contiguous continuation and idempotent in-memory successor receipts. Ending
an escort precedes a different service handover; the chain cannot swap workers.
