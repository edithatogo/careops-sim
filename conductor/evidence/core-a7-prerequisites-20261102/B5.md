A single-active bounded theatre uses actual B4 static team/space admission.
Acceptance, transfer departure/arrival, service start, incision, birth, completion
and room cleaning are distinct callbacks. Transfer never creates a birth. No
full theatre/postnatal dynamics or clinical policy is supplied. All policy/target
and service values in the tests are explicitly synthetic.

ObservedPolicyState is the only assessment input. Its elapsed wait is explicitly
anchored to the original theatre decision within this adapter. Queue priority
is a configured rank, independent of category numbering and target bounds.
Queued reassessment preserves the original decision clock; active service stays
non-interruptible. Policy identity/content cannot drift under the same revision.
Actual target metrics retain the immutable assessment tick/version used at each
callback; later policy changes cannot rewrite earlier metrics. Unknown targets
remain unknown; zero targets use direct elapsed comparison, without ratios.

Queue order is rank/decision/case ID. Due callback batches precede new external
boundary/policy events at that tick. Zero-time preparation/birth stages have
explicit incision/birth/completion/cleaning order; total service must be positive.
All arithmetic is checked before resource admission. Failed resource attempts
retain raw causes and actual attempt times, preserve claims and advance the
observation clock. Acceptance/departure/arrival/start/incision times retain the
separate transfer/travel/queue/preparation intervals. Historical retries do not
duplicate effects. Cancellation applies only to a queued procedure request; B6
owns any transfer compensation and ongoing clinical responsibility.

The coordinator merges global event times, consumes Birth callbacks, and performs
B4's responsibility audit after the complete global tick batch. This adapter does
not claim a distributed scheduler, dynamic staffing grant or durable restart.
