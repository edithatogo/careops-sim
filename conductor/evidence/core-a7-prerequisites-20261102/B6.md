service episodes to the qualified core TransferManager. Explicit destination
profiles declare resource identity, units and known opening intervals. Unknown
availability cannot grant; fallback destinations are allowlisted. Only the core
owns transfer state, logical claims, person ownership and physical location.
The coordinator retains identity episode entry/exit and physical capacity duties.
Physical vacancy alone cannot authorize reuse while an origin logical claim is held.
Maternal discharge does not implicitly discharge infants or remove relationships.

Every new applied effect (including causal buffer drainage) validates the actual
live source episode. Optional escorts require the declared same worker/booking/
chain over the actual departure-to-handover interval, including compensation.
Clone/validate/commit retains atomic rejection. Historical exact retries have no
new effects and do not require a still-open source episode. Compensation follows
core v1: rejection after physical arrival, before handover; nested diversion is
unsupported. No unqualified in-transit rerouting or distributed ownership is claimed.

The transfer manager's checkpoint round-trip is tested only for its core ledger;
this adapter does not claim durable domain/room/team restart. Resource units remain
explicit bed/cot/seat declarations, never interchangeable numerical capacity.
