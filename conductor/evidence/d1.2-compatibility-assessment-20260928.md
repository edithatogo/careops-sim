## Toolchain and compatibility governance

- Kairos Track 30's `conductor/toolchain-matrix.md` says Rust core MSRV 1.76,
  stable 1.95.x as of 2026-05-06, and a beta advisory lane (lines 31-45). This
  is stale against the September 2026 Rust 1.98.1 tested profile. Its validator
  still contains the 1.95 selector. Track 30 owns the matrix/workflow policy,
  while its spec excludes Cargo manifests and assigns MSRV policy to Track 13.
- Track 30's version-drop procedure requires at least two release cycles or six
  months notice, whichever is longer, along with a stated removal criterion,
  retained CI/smoke/waiver, and any exception ADR/waiver naming approver, expiry,
  affected users and compensating control (`conductor/toolchain-matrix.md:86-98`).
- Track 25's versioning compatibility contract treats existing Rust/API floors
  as protected promises. A floor change needs an exact affected-root ADR and a
  versioned transition; protected changes remain release-held until required
  policy, inventory and review artifacts align. Track 25's agent contract
  prohibits changing compatibility promises without an ADR.
- Track 30's recorded handoff is historical and does not review these candidate
  versions. Kairos Track 25 handoff still requires reviewer signoff at its
  recorded state. No Track 25/30/13 owner decision for this D1.2 scope was found.
- MLX is not in Track 30's Rust-only support matrix. Keep the Rust wrapper
  profile distinct from MLX C++/Metal/Xcode/Clang requirements; no MLX hardware
  or toolchain validation is claimed here.

The policy review was independently checked by bounded read-only agents against
Kairos Tracks 25 and 30. Their reports found no owner approval and no source
changes. This is coordinator evidence, not a maintainer signoff.

## Proposed disposition for owner review

1. Preserve Rust 1.76 for the tested existing core and its consumer baseline.
2. Treat Arrow 60 / Parquet 60 (1.88) and TOML 1.1 (1.85) as separate new-package
   or explicitly bounded-tool graphs, each with its own published floor,
   minimum/current locked consumer tests, and feature interoperability gates.
3. Keep Rayon optional and outside deterministic within-run scheduling. Resolve
   the current Criterion/Clap bench graph at 1.76 or formally scope a higher
   unpublished tool-only floor; do not describe the whole workspace as passing
   1.76 until the declared workspace targets pass.
4. Update the stale Track 30 stable selector/matrix and its validator only with
   Track 30/13 owner review; pin the tested stable baseline and dated canary as
   distinct lanes. A current registry maximum remains advisory until installed,
   resolved, and tested against supported targets.
5. Keep any existing-package MSRV increase gated by the Track 25 compatibility
   ADR, Track 30 transition rules, Track 13 toolchain/CI changes, and affected
   crate-owner review. This proposal grants none of those approvals.

Required owner decisions: (a) whether the current workspace-wide 1.76 promise
includes dev/benchmark targets or only published runtime packages; (b) whether
the bench graph should pin older compatible transitives or receive a scoped
tool-only floor; (c) which Kairos crate owns Arrow IPC/Parquet and the accepted
floor; (d) whether the outdated stable selector may be updated to the tested
1.98.1 baseline with a dated canary lane; and (e) Track 25/30/13/04 signoff on
any affected promises. Until resolved, manifests/pins remain unchanged and
D1.2 stays open.
