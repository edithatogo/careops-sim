# E0.1 coordinator acceptance — 2026-10-02

## Decision

Accept E0.1's behavior-first contracts and fixtures. Its three accepted leaves
cover (1) scenario-validation negative cases and stable diagnostics, (2) explicit
CareOps/Kairos/presentation ownership boundaries, and (3) a hand-computable
one-patient trace with conservation and capacity checks. Independent reviewers
identified test weaknesses in the schema and boundary leaves; exact assertions
were added and mutation probes then passed for all reviewed cases.
