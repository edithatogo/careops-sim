# E0.1 module-boundary contract — leaf acceptance (2026-10-02)

## Decision and scope

Accept `E0.1.boundary.module_boundary` for the logical ownership/dependency
contract and its integrity tests. CareOps ED behavior, site policy, runner and
exports are parent-owned. Reusable Kairos DES/ABM/state/time/RNG components are
upstream-owned. Parent layers may call public Kairos interfaces; reusable engine
crates cannot depend on ED/site policy or presentation; presentation is deferred
and consumes public interfaces or exported artifacts.

This leaf verifies contract data only. It does not assert that planned Cargo
packages exist, that current manifests conform, or that the Rust API/runner paths
are frozen. E0.2 must map logical modules to the actual packages/boundaries and
test the real Cargo dependency graph before acceptance of that implementation.
