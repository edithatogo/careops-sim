# E0.3 source, assumption and measurement inventory — 2026-10-02

## Disposition and method

This inventory reuses the accepted P1/P2 source records and report 26–30
reconciliation; it does not create a second copy of source data or repeat the
completed broad research intake. The P1/P2 files named below contain source
revisions, source-byte hashes, exact locations/fields, population, period, units,
licence/access terms, transformations, independent readbacks and limitations.
Where a source does not support a generative input, that gap is retained. None of
the source summaries below becomes a generic or Cairns default.

## E0 executable assumptions and metric contract

E0 accepts only the synthetic one-work-item smoke contract in
[`e0-assumptions-and-boundary.md`](../design/ed/e0-assumptions-and-boundary.md)
and the runner contract. Its input `work_ticks` is invented test work, not an
empirical service duration. For each started item, `wait_ticks = start - arrival`;
for completed items, `elapsed_ticks = completion - arrival`. At the inclusive
horizon, only arrivals strictly before the horizon are counted; completion at the
horizon is complete, and later completion is unfinished. `unfinished = arrivals -
completed`. No arrival generator, acuity, pathway, LOS, warm-up exclusion,
replication interval, censoring adjustment, throughput rate, occupancy estimate,
or calibration target is implemented or reported.

The supplied fixture's one-item outcome is hand-computable and seed-stable, but
E0 consumes no randomness. It has no empirical generic profile and no Cairns
transfer claim. Rust 1.76.0 is the stated minimum toolchain and was exercised on
this host; additional operating systems, release packaging, hosted CI, and a
clean external clone/submodule fetch are not qualified here.

## Frozen downstream boundaries

- Report 29's ward-level patient movements and capacity scenarios do not identify
  finite ED-eligible bed offers. Later ED behavior must model destination
  compatibility, offer lifetime/persistence/withdrawal, unused offers, competing
  demand, and retain ED occupancy until actual physical departure.
- The five ambulance clocks listed above remain distinct; the E0 runner contains
  none of them and makes no fleet, offload, handover, receiver-staff, or community
  response claim.
- Generic example profiles may use named synthetic assumptions when clearly
  labelled. Evidence-backed values need their own source record, exact population,
  period, units, source/licence and applicability review. Cairns requires its own
  mapping and local source/elicitation evidence.
- P4 produces validated example data. Loading those examples through the full
  model/calibration runner remains the P5 gate.

## E0.3 completion evidence

This crosswalk is the E0.3 output. Source details remain in the cited P1/P2
records rather than being duplicated. Manual review confirmed that every
reported value in the E0 executable example is synthetic, every unavailable
parameter remains unknown or deferred, and the public sources are not treated as
proof of generic/Cairns applicability.
