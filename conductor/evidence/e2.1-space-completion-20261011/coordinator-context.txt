## Separate lifecycles

A named ED space has capacity1 and one lifecycle state: ready, reserved,
occupied, dirty_waiting, or cleaning. Aggregate usable space follows the existing
known physical/open/staffed capacity contract. Declared named units must agree
with those counts. Do not represent an invalid static zero-capacity profile as
valid dynamic closure; explicitly configured calendar unavailability can block
work without altering valid static capacity. IDs follow the staff ASCII1..64
rule. Cap1024 named spaces,100000 patients and100000 reservation attempts.
The native aggregate event budget is reviewed in E2.2 before construction.

ED occupancy begins at a real space grant and ends only at physical departure.
An admission decision, offer allocation, staff handover or queued diagnostics
never releases the patient's ED space. Space occupancy/reservation is separate
from a work claim. Each work item grants one unit of one resource, including
one named staff member where configured; no simultaneous staff-plus-space or
staff-plus-equipment atomic request. Preserve claim and waiting history.

On departure, a configured positive-duration cleaning task makes space dirty
immediately. It stays unavailable throughout cleaner wait and cleaning. Select
one eligible cleaner under the staff calendar/zone/skill policy. Cleaning starts
only at the actual cleaner grant; no implicit unstaffed timer. Cleaner work obeys
DrainActive. At cleaning completion, release cleaner claim and make space ready.
A busy/unavailable cleaner is normal queued work, not malformed configuration.
No patient may enter a dirty space while cleaning has not started. Initial spaces
are ready unless the case explicitly supplies a valid initial dirty lifecycle.

For diagnostic cases, reserve one diagnostic room first, then queue one named
staff work claim. The patient retains ED space. Waiting for staff holds no staff
claim; a held diagnostic-room reservation is explicit in output and persists
through that work's completion. On completion release room and staff separately.
Patients waiting for a diagnostic room hold no room claim. This fixed ordering
is the reviewed fixture approximation; no circular or atomic acquisition is
implied. Work, staff wait and room wait remain separate. E2.2 must integrate all
these lifecycles into one authoritative runtime rather than simulate them twice.

## Reservations

A reservation attempt names ID, patient, space, start and expiry with start<expiry;
start is within [0,H] and expiry may exceed H. At its start it succeeds only if
the space is ready and patient-space compatibility holds. Competing attempts
are processed by start tick then reservation ID bytewise. Losers are explicitly
rejected with current-state reason, never queued or silently redirected. An
occupied, dirty, cleaning or already-reserved space cannot be reserved. Overlap
between attempts is valid input with a deterministic rejected attempt outcome.

An accepted reservation blocks all other patients during [start,expiry). Its
named eligible patient may consume it once, converting reserved to occupied.
If unused it expires, returning ready space. After consumption its scheduled
expiry is a stale no-op; it must never release the resulting occupancy. Expiry wins at the same tick as
consumption; the former owner can then compete as an ordinary waiting patient.
At one tick: finish work/physical departures and cleaning, apply calendar changes
and reservation expiries, process reservation attempts, then grant spaces and
eligible work. A departing space requiring cleaning becomes dirty before any new
reservation/grant. Reserved owners consume their own compatible spaces first;
unreserved waiting patients order by arrival tick then patient ID, choosing the
lowest compatible ready space ID. No preemption of an existing space occupant.
A new reservation starting at the previous expiry may win before ordinary grants;
this finite synthetic policy does not establish general starvation freedom.

## Dispatch and calendar semantics

At each relevant tick: complete work/release claims, apply calendar changes, then
grant new work. A request is eligible after ready_tick. Visit waiting requests by
urgency descending, ready_tick ascending, request_id bytewise ascending. For each,
choose the lowest staff_id among idle staff whose assigned_zone exactly matches,
whose skills include every required skill, and who are inside a shift and outside
all breaks. A higher-priority request with no eligible staff does not block a
lower-priority eligible one. Repeat until no eligible pair exists. Staff may hold
at most one active work assignment; waiting consumes no staff claim.

Default fixture in-flight policy is explicit version1 DrainActive: calendar
unavailability forbids new grants but active work continues to its actual end.
Do not shrink a Flow resource below active claims or manufacture a failed grant.
At a break or shift end, record active work overlapping that interval. Distinguish
break_overlap_ticks from work outside all shifts (shift_overtime_ticks). Their
union is scheduled_unavailable_work_ticks; disjoint shift/break rules prevent
double-counting. Do not invent a postponed break or pretend the policy proves
roster/clinical compliance. This is an explicit model approximation.

At an interval endpoint, half-open rules apply: no new grant at shift end/break
start; grants may start at shift start/break end. Work completing at such a boundary
releases normally; a replacement can start only if the resulting calendar permits.
A boundary at H still affects state/grants but contributes no time beyond H.
Handover and urgency preemption are mandatory separate E2.1.interrupt behaviors;
DrainActive here does not waive them. Cleaning/reservation is E2.1.space.

## Explicit handover

Handover is a configured domain event, not an automatic Flow lease transfer.
For these fixtures it names logical episode, tick, incumbent and replacement.
It is allowed only for an active consenting episode on the named incumbent.
Replacement must satisfy the same zone/skill/calendar rules at the actual grant.
It may be busy or unavailable when the instruction occurs; the continuation waits
without holding a claim until it becomes eligible.
At a handover tick, stop/checkpoint the old segment, release its exact active
claim once, preserve logical episode and remaining work, then enqueue the new
segment for the named replacement. It can wait without holding either staff.

Do not simultaneously retain an old claim and wait for a replacement claim.

"""Local bounded gate capture; no dispatch or general harness claim."""
import datetime
import hashlib
import json
import os
from pathlib import Path
import re
import subprocess
import sys
import time

ROOT = Path(__file__).resolve().parents[2]
GATES = {
    "fixtures": ["python3", "-B", "-m", "unittest", "discover", "-s", "tests", "-p", "test_e21_space.py", "-v"],
    "context": ["python3", "-B", "tools/context.py", "check"],
    "mvp": ["python3", "-B", "tools/mvp.py", "check"],
}
SOURCES = ["conductor/design/ed/e2-space.json", "tests/test_e21_space.py", "conductor/design/ed/e2-space-crosswalk.md"]

def hashes():
    return {p: hashlib.sha256((ROOT / p).read_bytes()).hexdigest() if (ROOT / p).is_file() else None for p in SOURCES}

def write_new(path, value):
    with path.open("x") as stream:
        json.dump(value, stream, indent=2)
        stream.write("\n")

def main():
    if len(sys.argv) != 2 or sys.argv[1] not in GATES:
        raise SystemExit("choose fixtures, context, or mvp")
    gate = sys.argv[1]
    out = ROOT / ".artifacts/mvp/E2.1.space.coordinator/logs"
    out.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    stem = f"{gate}-{time.time_ns()}-{os.getpid()}"
    log = out / (stem + ".log")
    record = {"gate": gate, "argv": GATES[gate], "cwd": str(ROOT),
              "head": subprocess.check_output(["git", "rev-parse", "HEAD"], cwd=ROOT, text=True).strip(),
              "python": sys.version, "start": datetime.datetime.now(datetime.timezone.utc).isoformat(),
              "source_hashes": hashes(), "log": str(log.relative_to(ROOT)), "status": "started"}
    write_new(out / (stem + "-started.json"), record)
    env = dict(os.environ, PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE="1")
    command_exit_code = None
    with log.open("x") as stream:
        if gate == "fixtures" and not (ROOT / SOURCES[1]).is_file():
            stream.write("Gate refused: declared test file is missing.\n")
            exit_code = 2
            record["gate_error"] = "Refused: declared test file is missing"
        else:
            command_exit_code = subprocess.run(GATES[gate], cwd=ROOT, env=env, stdout=stream, stderr=subprocess.STDOUT).returncode
            exit_code = command_exit_code
    content = log.read_bytes()
    observed = re.search(rb"Ran (\d+) tests?", content)
    if gate == "fixtures" and exit_code == 0 and (not observed or int(observed.group(1)) == 0):
        exit_code = 2
        record["gate_error"] = "Successful command did not report a nonzero test count"
    after = hashes()
    if after != record["source_hashes"]:
        exit_code = 2
        record["gate_error"] = "Owned source changed during command"
    record.update(status="finished", end=datetime.datetime.now(datetime.timezone.utc).isoformat(),
                  exit_code=exit_code, command_exit_code=command_exit_code, log_sha256=hashlib.sha256(content).hexdigest(), source_hashes_after=after)
    result = out / (stem + "-result.json")
    write_new(result, record)
    print(json.dumps({"exit_code": exit_code, "receipt": str(result.relative_to(ROOT)), "log": record["log"]}))
    if exit_code:
        print(content.decode(errors="replace")[-3000:])
    return exit_code

if __name__ == "__main__":
    raise SystemExit(main())
