## Phase evidence summary

- **E2.1 Operational Constraints (PR #106, PR #107)**:
  - Strict shift reductions conforming to the Kairos shrink contract: roster reductions that drop active in-flight work are rejected atomically.
  - Idle staff breaks do not evict busy staff.
  - Space occupancy, cleaning, and reservation lifecycles are decoupled from staff claims.
  - One bay holds one patient; blocked diagnostics and boarding retain bay hold.
  - Handover transfers assignment without dropping in-flight work.
  - Preemption requires strictly higher clinical urgency (ATS 1-5) and explicit consent, preserving elapsed work ticks in `WorkAccount`.

- **E2.2 Dispatch, Route Fidelity, and Phased Claims (PR #108, PR #110)**:
  - Dispatch candidate selection: strictly orders by clinical urgency (ATS 1-5), then FIFO within acuity (`enqueue_tick`), then deterministic tie-breaking (`patient_id`, `task_id`).
  - Phased resource claims: explicit separate occupancy/reservation lifecycle preceding staff claims (`execute_phased_claim`); implicit atomic multi-resource acquisition fails closed (`claim_atomic_multi_resource` returns `AtomicMultiResourceUnsupported`).
  - Execution fidelity: Macro mode incurs zero hidden transit overhead; Micro mode computes exact edge transit duration (`distance_m / speed_m_per_s`) with validation of inaccessible/unreachable edges.
  - Procedure staffing: multi-person sedation, resuscitation, and assisted transfer are rejected (`UnsupportedMultiRole`) unless configured with a reviewed composite team approximation.

- **E2.3 Composed Fixture, ST001–ST009 Boundaries, and MVP Runner (PR #111, PR #112)**:
  - Composed ED fixture (`CompositeEdFixture`) integrating named zones (`zone.triage`, `zone.resus`, `zone.acute`, `zone.subacute`), treatment spaces, and staff shifts.
  - Evaluates simultaneous arrivals, priority dispatch, and preemption/resumption.
  - Invariant verification: zero double allocation, zero leaked capacity, no starvation hidden by censoring, patient count conservation (arrivals = completed + unfinished), and work conservation.
  - Boundary cases ST001–ST009:
    - **ST001**: Destination-compatible bed offers; incompatible offers rejected.
    - **ST002**: Unused offers policy with explicit lifetime and persistence (no immediate expiry default).
    - **ST003**: Boarder retained bay occupancy until physical departure is complete.
    - **ST004**: Recurrent care tasks supported without reserving full-time 1:1 nurse by default.
    - **ST005**: Saturated offload: receiving bay saturation blocks ambulance offload and delays crew release.
    - **ST006**: Receiver staff sensitivity: offload delay depends on receiving nurse availability.
    - **ST007**: Multi-person procedures require dedicated composite teams.
    - **ST008**: Prohibits ungrounded causal claims of inpatient ward discharge policies from external arrivals.
    - **ST009**: Prohibits implicit default deterioration hazard without explicit configuration.
  - Headless MVP scenario runner:
    - Nominal scenario (`crates/careops-ed/examples/nominal_mvp.json`): 2 acute bays, 0.4 mean wait ticks, 100% completion.
    - Constrained scenario (`crates/careops-ed/examples/constrained_mvp.json`): 1 acute bay, 4.2 mean wait ticks, demonstrating controlled capacity comparison.
    - Malformed scenario rejection (`crates/careops-ed/examples/malformed_mvp.json`): exits with non-zero exit code and diagnostic message.
    - Fixed-seed repeatability: repeated runs produce identical byte-for-byte JSON output.
    - CSV summary formatting (`run_summary_to_csv`) and capacity comparison oracle (`compare_capacity`).

## Manual check verification (Phase E2 exit criteria)

Manual check: inspect staff occupancy and patient availability across a shift and interruption.
1. **Shift break & shrink across active work**:
   - `StaffShift::begin_break` reduces claimable capacity while keeping active staff busy without eviction.
   - `StaffShift::shrink_scheduled` rejected with `CapacityInUse` when active work + on_break exceeds proposed scheduled count.
   - Verified in `crates/careops-ed/tests/e21_operational_constraints.rs:breaks_and_shift_shrink_keep_in_flight_work`.
2. **Preemption interruption and resumption**:
   - Patient 1 (ATS 5) active for 2 ticks in bay. Urgent Patient 2 (ATS 1) arrives.
   - Preemption interrupts Patient 1: bay and nurse yielded to Patient 2, but Patient 1's 2 elapsed ticks are preserved in `WorkAccount`.
   - Patient 2 finishes 2 ticks. Patient 1 resumes and completes remaining 4 ticks (total 6 ticks work conserved).
   - Verified in `crates/careops-ed/tests/e23_composite_fixture_and_boundaries.rs:test_composite_ed_fixture_preemption_and_work_conservation`.
3. **Staff handover**:
   - Nurse assignment transfers to incoming nurse without dropping in-flight work or resetting elapsed progress.
   - Verified in `crates/careops-ed/tests/e21_operational_constraints.rs:handover_moves_the_assignment_without_dropping_work`.
