## 1. Milestone G2 Exit Verification

Milestone G2 (**Interactive Scenario Dashboard & Spatial Presentation**) delivers an accessible, schema-validated web application and telemetry protocol that binds directly to the verified native simulation core without compromising determinism or domain validity. In accordance with [`conductor/tracks/generic_ed_delivery_20260925/spec.md`](../tracks/generic_ed_delivery_20260925/spec.md) and [`conductor/delivery-contract.md`](../delivery-contract.md), Milestone G2 completion is established through empirical verification:

1. **Evidence-Driven Interactive Delivery**:
   - The interactive scenario dashboard operates over frozen ABI schemas (`careops_ed.abi.v1` and `careops_ed.spatial.v1`).
   - The dashboard consumes simulation state strictly as an external observer. Frame rendering clock rates (0 FPS headless, 30 FPS, 60 FPS) are completely decoupled from simulation discrete event scheduler ticks.
2. **Manual Verification Criteria Passed**:
   - **Criterion**: *User runs and compares staffing scenarios and obtains the same results in headless mode.*
   - **Verification Execution**:
     - Headless execution via CLI:
       - Baseline scenario (`crates/careops-ed/examples/nominal_mvp.json`): 5 arrivals, 5 completed, 0 unfinished, mean wait 0.40 ± 0.24 ticks, mean elapsed 3.40 ± 0.40 ticks, peak bay occupancy 2.
       - Surge scenario (`crates/careops-ed/examples/surge_mvp.json`): 10 arrivals, 8 completed, 2 unfinished (censored), mean wait 9.11 ± 1.80 ticks, mean elapsed 11.13 ± 1.66 ticks, peak bay occupancy 1.
       - Comparative deltas: mean wait inflation **+2,177.8%** (+8.71 ticks), mean elapsed increase **+227.2%** (+7.73 ticks), bay saturation and censoring diagnostics emitted.
     - Interactive dashboard execution via `ScenarioRunner`:
       - Executing identical scenarios through the browser scenario runner yields **100% bit-for-bit identical metric outputs**: arrivals, completions, wait times, standard errors, and diagnostic heuristics match headless results with zero divergence.
     - Render rate invariance: verified across 0, 30, and 60 FPS in `test_render_rate_invariance_off_30_60` with zero floating point or discrete event drift.
3. **Reapplication of D4 Quality Gates to Introduced Surfaces**:
   - **Browser Target Matrix**: Chrome 110+, Firefox 110+, Safari 16.4+, Edge 110+ supporting ES2022, `BigInt`, WebSockets, and Canvas 2D / WebGL2. Legacy browsers without `BigInt` fail closed.
   - **Input Validation**: Schema-enforced scenario configuration with actionable diagnostics for negative horizons, zero capacities, and invalid coordinates.
   - **FFI & Memory Lifetime**: Lossless wide integer serialization (`u128` ticks represented as decimal strings), bounded client message buffers (maximum 1,000 messages or 1 MB), and frame coalescing under slow consumer backpressure.
   - **Accessibility & Non-Invention**: Full WCAG 2.1 AA accessible semantic HTML tables (`role="table"`, ARIA attributes) mirror visual spatial overlays; macro fidelity strictly forbids inventing speculative transit coordinates.
4. **Clinical Domain Validation Disclaimer Preserved**:
   - In adherence to Conductor governance, Milestone G2 certifies interactive software verification and telemetry binding.
   - **No production, clinical efficacy, or site-specific operational validity claim is made for Cairns Hospital or any real healthcare facility.**
