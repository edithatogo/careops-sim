## 3. Lookahead Certification vs Empirical Quantiles

A critical specification requirement of Task E7.1:
> *"Certify each cross-LP minimum causal advance, including same-time cycles; empirical duration means/quantiles are never lookahead guarantees."*

### Mathematical Certification Rules
Every cross-LP directed edge $(i, j)$ requires a certified non-zero lookahead $\mathcal{L}_{ij} > 0$:

$$\mathcal{L}_{ij} = \max\left(\frac{d_{\min}(i, j)}{v_{\max}},\ t_{\text{protocol\_floor}}(i, j)\right)$$

Where:
- $d_{\min}(i, j)$ is the physical minimum geodesic distance between the boundary of LP $i$ and LP $j$ (in meters).
- $v_{\max}$ is the maximum permissible physical transit speed ($2.5\text{ m/s}$ for clinical staff).
- $t_{\text{protocol\_floor}}(i, j)$ is the certified minimum duration mandated by clinical protocol (e.g. 5 seconds for triage handover).

### Invariant Prohibition on Empirical Distributions
- **Prohibition**: Empirical duration distributions (such as an observed mean triage duration of $35\text{ minutes}$ or a 5th-percentile quantile of $P_{05} = 4\text{ minutes}$) **MUST NEVER** be used as lookahead guarantees.
- **Rationale**: Stochastic empirical distributions permit draws approaching zero or tail variations that violate causality during execution. Conservative CMB execution requires absolute causal lower bounds.

## 4. Zero-Lookahead Cycle Failure & Deadlock Prevention

## 5. In-Flight Message Accounting & Global Virtual Time (GVT)

In parallel execution, events traveling between LPs are in transit. To prevent causality violations, the Global Virtual Time (GVT) consensus calculation strictly accounts for both local clocks and in-flight messages:

$$\text{GVT} = \min\left(\min_{i \in \text{LPs}} T_i,\ \min_{m \in \text{InFlight}} t(m)\right)$$

### Verified GVT Properties
1. **In-Flight Bound**: When LP clocks are at $40\text{s}, 45\text{s}, 50\text{s}, 60\text{s}$ and an in-flight message has timestamp $35\text{s}$, $\text{GVT} = 35\text{s}$. The system never advances beyond the oldest in-flight message.
2. **Monotonic Progression**: Upon message delivery, $\text{GVT}$ advances to $40\text{s}$, guaranteeing $\text{GVT}_{k+1} \ge \text{GVT}_k$.
3. **Safe Horizon**: Workers execute local events only up to their certified safe horizon ($T_{\text{safe}} = \min_{j} (T_j + \mathcal{L}_{ji})$).
