## 2. Real Worker Communication & CMB Protocol

### Message Envelopes
Inter-LP communication operates exclusively through typed messages without shared mutable state:
1. **`RemoteEvent`**: Represents clinical patient handoffs:
   - `event_id`: Monotonic sequence identifier.
   - `source_lp`: Transmitting LP (`LpId`).
   - `dest_lp`: Receiving LP (`LpId`).
   - `timestamp_ns`: Simulation timestamp ($t \ge \text{LocalClock} + \mathcal{L}$).
   - `patient_id`: Unique stable patient identifier.
   - `payload`: Clinical acuity and treatment summary.
2. **`NullMessage`**: Advertises causal lower bounds to downstream neighbors:
   - `source_lp`: Sending LP.
   - `dest_lp`: Receiving LP.
   - `safe_time_ns`: Guaranteed lower bound on all future events from `source_lp` ($\text{LocalClock} + \mathcal{L}$).

### Safe Time Calculation & Deadlock Freedom
Each LP maintains an input channel horizon table. The safe horizon before which events can be committed without causality risk is:
$$T_{\text{safe}}(i) = \min_{j \in \text{InNeighbors}(i)} \left(T_{\text{channel}}(j \to i)\right)$$

Whenever an LP's local queue is empty or advances its clock, it broadcasts a `NullMessage` with timestamp $\text{LocalClock} + \mathcal{L}_{ij}$ to all downstream neighbors. Because all cycles have certified positive lookahead ($\sum \mathcal{L} > 0$), null message timestamps strictly advance monotonically, mathematically precluding CMB deadlock.

Total System Beds: **24 beds** strictly partitioned across the 4 LPs. Zero cross-LP lock contention.
