## Coordinator review

The Draft 2020-12 proposal distinguishes physical, open and staffed treatment
space counts; represents unknown counts explicitly without treating them as
available; models equipment/monitoring capability, named locations/zones and
optional route distances in metres. Both examples are labeled synthetic only,
not empirical and not recommended ED defaults. No drawing, CAD file or geometry
is required. The schema is a proposed shape and does not freeze E0/C0 APIs.

This leaf validates individual record shape. It does not enforce arithmetic
relationships such as staffed count being no greater than open count and open
count being no greater than physical count, nor route endpoint references across
records. Those remain explicit follow-up checks for the approved validator and
model-level consumer validation; no such validation is claimed here.
