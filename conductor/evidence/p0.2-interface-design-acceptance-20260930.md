# P0.2 ED parameter interface acceptance — 2026-09-30

**Decision:** accept the P0.2 **design interface** for 101 stable ED parameter IDs.
This accepts ID/consumer/key/unit/value-role/profile applicability contracts,
the parameter record schema, the static capacity/location binding schema and
their validators. It does not accept empirical values, fitted distributions,
clinical policy defaults, Rust runtime behavior or the functional ED MVP.

## Review and disposition

- Six independent E0/C0 role packets reviewed every applicable row. The
  101-row [resolved response](../../model-inputs/ed/schema/p0.2-owner-review-resolved.csv)
  preserves their identities, sessions, source bases, votes and evidence
  locators, and records a dated coordinator decision for each row.
- Three further bounded row reviews and two independent structured-matrix
  reviews found stage, role, key and copied-gate errors. The coordinator
  corrected them and independently checked the joined result. The final
  independent audit found no remaining design-interface blocker after the
  registry's graph-edge meaning and walkthrough's per-field time-unit wording
  were reconciled.
- The [accepted matrix](../../model-inputs/ed/schema/parameter-usage-matrix.json)
  has 29 `accept`, 61 `revise` and 11 `defer` decisions. Row 62 changed from the
  provisional `accept` to `revise`: its stable ID is retained, while the
  resolved key means versioned graph-edge length in metres. Deferred rows are
  excluded from E2 profiles until their named re-entry triggers are met.
  The original proposal, provisional disposition and three worker chunks
  remain as review lineage, not competing accepted contracts.

## Cross-cutting contracts

1. CareOps owns ED clinical configuration keys; Kairos 03/22 own Flow/runtime
   and scenario orchestration semantics. Per-field numeric units and category
   versions are explicit.
2. ED durations normalize to decimal seconds; ADR-0004 defines checked
   one-nanosecond `u128` ticks for the initial profile. A global time-unit
   switch is removed in favor of `time_fields.source_unit` metadata.
3. Relative generic schedules start at zero. Timestamped traces preserve
   occurrence/recorded/message lineage, source precision and explicit zone or
   offset. Naive local recurrence remains disabled until a tested adapter.
4. Named zones/locations and staff/resource bindings are E2 minimum inputs
   even in Macro mode. Micro route edges are metres and speeds are metres per
   second; geometry import and visual playback remain later work.
5. Generative inputs, observed targets and source metadata are distinct.
   Boarding is an observed boundary outcome, with only a separately identified
   residual delay eligible for generation. Finite bed offers and optional
   external competing demand remain separate from ED-generated admissions.
6. The E2 configuration includes one `experiment.run_seed` u64, matching
   pinned Kairos CLI `base_seed: u64`; named stream derivation is later Track
   01 work. Required versus conditional versus deferred inputs are explicit in
   the accepted matrix; no empirical defaults follow from this decision.

The [patient/staff walkthrough](p0.2-v2-patient-staff-walkthrough-20260930.md)
found a stable ID for every consumed synthetic input. It also records that
profile-level required-task membership is not proven by the static binding
validator when a task has no required resource class.
