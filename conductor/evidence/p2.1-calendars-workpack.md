## Affected model fields

Keep the following unset or explicitly unknown until source-defined inputs and a
reviewed behavior contract are available:

- Roster intervals, actual presence, absence/redeployment, and their time zone,
  role/grade, location, and effective times (`ed.resources.rosters`).
- Planned/observed break intervals, missed/postponed status, replacement/coverage,
  and any capacity effect (`ed.resources.breaks`).
- Staff task eligibility, role/skill eligibility, location assignment, and
  available actions at a shift or break state change.
- Treatment of an in-flight task at a shift or break boundary, including
  continuation, suspension, handover, and eventual resumption.

The resources contract snapshot leaves `ed.resources.rosters` and
`ed.resources.breaks` null. The prospective `p2-task-observation-protocol`
requests boundary and in-flight task state only when observed; it is an
acquisition checklist, not evidence that those fields exist or a behavior rule.
The P2 interruption workpack likewise distinguishes observed handover from
inference and does not define a handover policy.
