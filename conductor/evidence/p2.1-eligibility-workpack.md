## Acquisition fields for a future readback

The prospective protocol calls for a stable pseudonymous staff identifier; role/skill credentials and effective interval; shift/presence and location; persistent assignment with start/end and observed/source-defined status; timestamped decision opportunity and trigger; active task before the trigger; eligible actions with an explicit unknown/uncaptured category and a basis for each relation; and observed response. Preserve source bytes or governed query/version and hash in the restricted evidence environment. These are requested evidence fields, not claims that such data are currently present.

**Boundary:** eligible-action snapshots are not available in the supplied records. Choice and priority findings do not complete them. There is no eligibility edge established by this map, and no claim of complete choice-set coverage.
