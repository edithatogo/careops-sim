## Prospective observation needs

Use the accepted `p2-task-observation-protocol` as a prospective acquisition checklist, not as evidence that these fields exist in current systems. Capture prompt identifiers and sources separately from staff-initiated switches and observed response categories; stable task identifiers and explicit parent/interruptor links; task pause, completion, and resumption events with source confidence; decision opportunities and eligible actions, including unknown/uncaptured alternatives; the first subsequent task; eventual same-task resumption or explicit unknown; and observation-end censoring. Preserve role, site/profile, time coverage, missingness, source/version, and clock-quality metadata. Record original source bytes or governed extraction query/version and hash in the restricted evidence environment.

The available sources do not jointly supply this structure. The task-choice abstract lacks a complete choice set and task lineage; the interruption abstract lacks individual ancestry, first subsequent task, eventual resumption, and latency. Acquisition requires authorized direct observation or governed event-log extraction and independent readback. Until then, chronology and local deployment applicability remain unknown.
