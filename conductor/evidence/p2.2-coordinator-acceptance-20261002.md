The P2.2 primary evidence supports only bounded claims:

- Huguet et al. report anonymized staff location/activity analysis and hourly aggregated same-tag Euclidean displacement at one French ED. It does not establish an operational route graph, accessible connectivity, route-choice alternatives or a transferable speed distribution.
- The AMR-Hub pinned code is an open geometry-extraction method example, not a CareOps ED drawing or clinically validated graph.
- Johnson et al. describe grouped self-interruption examples that include retrieval of misplaced supplies; the reported 7/67 self rows are separate margins and neither count is a supply-trip count.
- Supplied work-system evidence and the remaining primary-source records identify interruption, task and workload structures but do not provide complete observed trajectories with activity-labelled transit, waiting, active work, origin/destination, route, assistance mode and resumption timestamps.

## Explicit remaining gaps

- Generic ED as-operated layout, stable location IDs, coordinate units/transform, door/access restrictions and operationally reachable graph.
- Directly observed route choices with the complete reachable alternative set, by movement mode and trip purpose.
- Mode-stratified obstacle-constrained speeds and patient/staff assistance needs.
- Supply retrieval episodes with a distinct purpose, item, origin/destination, exposure denominator, timing and availability outcome.
- An observed staff-trip/interruption trace that separates active work, transit, waiting, interruption, handover and resumption at event level.
- Local operational approval or a Cairns as-operated register. This is not required to create a generic synthetic profile and must not be inferred from architectural symbols.

### Manual evidence check

The available records do not expose a row-level staff trajectory from which every interval of a real trip/interrupted task can be reconstructed. The review therefore treats those intervals as **unidentified**, rather than labelling elapsed time as transit/work/wait or inventing an unobserved rule. This is the evidence-level check: one cannot explain the full real episode from the supplied public sources. The acquisition path is direct observation or a governed de-identified event source with the fields in `p2-task-observation-protocol.json`. P2.3 may use clearly labelled synthetic examples to test representation, but those cannot be promoted to observed behavior.
