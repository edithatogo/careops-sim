## Hand-worked representation check

`p23-synthetic-accounting.json` is explicitly synthetic test data. A directed
one-way edge of 18.5 m with a test speed of 1.0 m/s yields 18.5 s; reverse
reachability is not inferred and the closed edge is rejected. Queue wait (4 s),
active work (12 s), travel (18.5 s), and interruption pause (3 s) sum to one
37.5 s Micro interval. A Macro elapsed interval of the same length is compared
with that decomposition and cannot be added to it. Macro executes without a
route graph. These invented numbers test representation and accounting only;
they are not observations, ranges, generic ED defaults or walking-speed advice.

The P2/P3 join remains open for actual DES service/queue versus ABM travel,
work, waiting and interruption semantics, dependence, identification and
sampling order. No real as-operated ED graph, reachable route, speed, route
preference, staff decision policy or generic clinical behavior has been
established. Cairns evidence remains separate.
