## Integrated contract and implementation

The schema defines a version-1 synthetic data-only pack distinct from E0 executable scenario JSON. It requires relative decimal-second time values, an explicit arrival table, configurable acuity scale categories and case-mix table, resource and staffing calendars, the accepted P0.2 capacity/location structure, a nonempty directed route graph, finite discrete synthetic distributions, and explicit initialization. Occupants may validly have no active remaining work. P4.2 owns probability sums, interval ordering/coverage, count reconciliation, identifier references, resource feasibility, and other cross-field semantics. P5 owns full-model loading and consumption.

## Review and correction

That condition was removed, and a regression now proves an occupant with no active work is accepted. The schema was then expanded to require acuity scale and case-mix inputs because ED arrival counts alone do not describe the planned ED demand cohorts. Route graphs now require at least one edge.

Those fixtures are separate P4.1 leaves; the existing three E0 projections do not substitute for them.
