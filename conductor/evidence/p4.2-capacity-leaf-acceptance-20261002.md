## Scope and interpretation

The synthetic cases distinguish physical, open, and staffed counts; retain a
known closed bay in physical capacity while excluding it from open capacity; and
exercise the accepted surge pack's changing open/staffed calendar. Unknown
availability remains `null` with an explicit reason. The capacity schema accepts
that unknown evidence record, but the semantic validator excludes it from a
runnable profile because a runnable calendar requires numeric availability.
Changing unknown availability to numeric/open values fails the structural
schema. No unknown value is converted to physical capacity or treated as
available.

All values are synthetic. The cases add no empirical, clinical, operational, or
Cairns claim and do not modify the accepted P4 profiles, schema, or validators.
