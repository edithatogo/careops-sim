# P4.2 semantic consistency leaf acceptance — 2026-10-02

## Disposition

Accepted the bounded `P4.2.consistency` leaf after coordinator verification and
independent review. Two review rounds found and closed gaps: the validator now
requires full-horizon calendars for every declared resource bucket, reports
malformed resource capacity counts, and returns diagnostics for malformed
unhashable identifiers rather than raising `TypeError`.

## Scope

Added a callable semantic validator and explicit profile-path CLI for the
data-only P4 v1 envelope. It checks ordered interval coverage, per-interval and
per-mode case-mix totals/category support, finite discrete distribution support
and probability sums, capacity feasibility, resource/staffing calendar coverage,
initial occupants/work, and explicit synthetic provenance. The structural
validator remains a separate check. These profiles are synthetic examples, not
empirical ED estimates, fitted distributions, operational defaults, or Cairns
values. Runtime/model loading remains P5 work.
