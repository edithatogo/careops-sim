# P4.4 phase review and acceptance — 2026-10-03

## Disposition

P4 is accepted for its bounded objective: synthetic generic ED input shapes,
schema and semantic validation, user guidance, future Cairns acquisition mapping,
and frozen data-only handoff inventory for E1/C5. The phase establishes no
empirical generic ED values, fitted distributions, clinical validity, Cairns
applicability, runtime profile loading, or consumer acceptance.

## Exit criteria readback

- **P-R3, profile consistency checks:** met for the declared schema-v1 example
  shapes. P4.1 and P4.2 receipts record structural schema checks, conditional and
  interval semantics, support/probability checks, clock units, calendars,
  initialization, provenance, malformed/missing/censored cases, and capacity
  status examples. P4.3 records all three packs passing structural and semantic
  validation and the manifest's five exact-byte hashes. These are data validation
  results; they do not establish empirical distribution fit or runtime sampling.
- **P-R6, generic-to-Cairns mapping:** met as an acquisition and mapping plan.
  Local Cairns sources and values remain unknown. A reviewed schema/provenance/
  validator/manifest/acceptance-contract revision is required before representing
  any observed Cairns values.
- **Complete input shapes:** minimal, nominal, surge, malformed/missing/censored,
  capacity and manifest examples are synthetic and cover the supported schema-v1
  shapes. No public dataset is represented as a populated profile. The P4 plan
  permits linked sources or clearly labelled synthetic assumptions.
- **Manual interpretability:** the guide distinguishes value/evidence classes,
  ranges, assumptions, unknowns, limits and overrides without requiring source
  inspection. It documents that validators do not establish staff-to-resource
  feasibility or every cross-reference relation.
