## Manual exit traces

These are paper checks of the proposed contract, not runtime-test evidence.

1. Capacity one; low-priority work A has duration 10 and starts at tick 0. A
   high-priority, preempting work B of duration 2 arrives at tick 3. B evicts A;
   A has 3 elapsed and 7 remaining. B completes at tick 5. Under Suspend, A
   resumes for 7 ticks and completes at 12. Under Restart, A runs the original
   sampled duration 10 and completes at 15. Under Abort, A becomes terminal at
   tick 3 and never resumes. B completes at tick 5 in every case. A's original
   completion event at tick 10 is stale after eviction and must be a no-op.
2. A waiting request has an exclusive deadline at tick 5, the same tick a
   release makes capacity available. Boundary processing expires requests with
   deadline less than or equal to now before arbitration. Therefore the request
   times out and is never granted whether the release event or timeout token
   was inserted first; capacity remains free after release. This rule is local
   to Flow and does not reorder unrelated scheduler events.
