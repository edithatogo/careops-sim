## Review and checks

The four cases contain 16 operation snapshots plus four initial states:

1. With capacity two and equal-priority active victims A(sequence 1) and
   C(sequence 2), urgent B evicts only C. A remains active; C resumes its seven
   remaining ticks after B finishes and completes at tick 12.
2. Zero-duration work receives exactly one Grant and one Complete transition at
   tick 0, consuming the configured budget of two exactly.
3. With budget two, the first two same-tick transitions commit; the third returns
   `SameTickBudgetExceeded`, remains pending, and another call at tick 0 returns
   the same outcome without changing state.
4. With budget one, a transition at tick 0 and another at tick 1 each commit with
   a fresh count for the later tick.
