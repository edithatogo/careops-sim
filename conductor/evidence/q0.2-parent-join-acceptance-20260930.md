## Joined acceptance criteria

- Priority ascends numerically; equal-priority claims are FIFO; reprioritization
  retains admission sequence; cancellation is terminal and resubmission uses a
  distinct claim ID and new admission sequence (`B2`). Equal-priority claims do
  not preempt. Independent `can_preempt` and `preemptible(strategy)` flags remain
  separate.
- Deadline-at-T expires before grant in both command insertion orders;
  completion-at-T completes the holder before eviction/arbitration. Shrinking
  below active use rejects atomically; zero idle capacity is valid and later
  growth drains waiting work.
- Preemption chooses only an eligible strictly lower-urgency victim; equal
  victim priorities select the latest admission. The capacity-two oracle evicts
  only C while A remains active.
- On the 0/3/5 timeline, Suspend resumes A's seven remaining ticks and completes
  at 12 with 10 busy ticks; Abort terminates A at 3 with 3 busy and 7 remaining;
  Restart starts attempt 1 at 5, completes at 15, reuses the same duration draw,
  and records 13 cumulative busy ticks. Suspend elapsed accumulates within the
  attempt: A is elapsed 3 at resume; victim-tie C is elapsed 3 at tick 5 and 8
  at tick 10. Restart resets elapsed only for its new attempt.
- A zero-duration request grants and completes once at tick 0. The finite
  same-tick budget accepts exact-limit work, blocks over-limit work with pending
  state preserved, stays blocked on repeat calls at the same tick, resets after
  time advances, and counts delivered continuation notifications.
- At the same tick, cancel/reprioritize follows Scheduler
  (time, scheduler-priority, insertion-sequence) order for both equal-priority
  insertion orders; a differing Scheduler priority precedes insertion order.
  Scheduler priority remains separate from resource queue priority.
- The SimPy note distinguishes upstream-documented ordering/interruption/release
  behavior from local Kairos decisions and explicitly says Flow budget support
  is not implemented and its numeric default remains a Q1 decision.
- Required manual walkthroughs agree: the 0/3/5 strategy arithmetic above and
  timeout-at-release at deadline T expire before grant without time reversal.
