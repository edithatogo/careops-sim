| Domain | Families | Required content | Preferred representation |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **D01 Demand and arrivals** | 14 | annual activity, hourly/day/seasonal intensity, holidays, trend, surges, referral source, transfer arrivals, clustering/batches | empirical time-varying intensity; NHPP baseline only after diagnostics; renewal/Cox/empirical day-resampling if needed |
| **D02 Patient attributes and joint case mix** | 18 | age, sex, pregnancy, paediatric status, ATS, complaint, diagnosis, injury, comorbidity, frailty, mental health, isolation, interpreter, equity variables, rurality, prior use | empirical joint resampling or conditional generative model |
| **D03 Prehospital and ambulance** | 14 | ambulance share, arrival/triage/offload, handover, crew release, turnaround, offload capacity, review/escalation, retrieval, bypass | explicit queue/resource process; policy thresholds deterministic |
| **D04 Reception and triage** | 11 | registration/triage service, staff, ATS, reassessment, misclassification, streaming and bypass | positive service-time distribution + policy rules + conditional classification |
| **D05 Care pathways** | 16 | assessments, reviews, procedures, observation, consultation, repeated work, parallelism, discharge, short stay, death | state-dependent pathway graph with competing/parallel events |
| **D06 Physical/effective capacity** | 14 | resus, acute, fast-track, mental-health, isolation and short-stay spaces; surge; opening, cleaning, equipment, downtime | integer time-varying capacities + compatibility constraints |
| **D07 Workforce** | 17 | rosters by grade, breaks, absence, overtime, redeployment, skills, supervision, handover, productivity, interruptions | roster schedules + task-skill matrix + shift/staff random effects |
| **D08 Diagnostics/support** | 16 | pathology, imaging, transport, processing, reporting, consultation, pharmacy, porters, competing workload, opening hours | component queues and positive task durations |
| **D09 Queue/resource allocation** | 13 | urgency, ageing, overrides, clinician allocation, resource bundles, pre-emption, reservation, overflow, blocking, tie-breaking | explicit deterministic/probabilistic operating rules |
| **D10 Clinical trajectories/safety** | 10 | latent clinical state, deterioration, recovery, reassessment, adverse events, mortality, treatment response | multi-state/competing-risk process when in scope |
| **D11 Patient/carer decisions** | 10 | LWBS, departure after treatment starts, balking, perceived wait, refusal, alternatives, reattendance, carers/transport | time-to-event/choice models |
| **D12 Staff/organisation behaviour** | 10 | task choice, risk tolerance, communication, cooperation, bed allocation, discharge priority, crowding response, learning | decision rules or locally fitted choice models |
| **D13 Admission/boarding/inpatient flow** | 17 | admission, specialty, acceptance, ward capacity, staffing, compatibility, elective/non-ED demand, LOS, discharge, cleaning, transport, boarding | ward-level DES boundary |
| **D14 Discharge/external constraints** | 11 | readiness, medicines, transport, family, home care, rehabilitation, residential care, mental health, repatriation, social barriers | external queues/time-to-event processes |
| **D15 Networks/disruptions** | 11 | interhospital choice/acceptance, retrieval, diversion, mutual aid, epidemic/weather, failures, workforce shock | optional network/scenario module |
| **D16 Intervention settings** | 12 | staffing, streaming, diagnostics, senior assessment, discharge coordination, SSU, offload, beds, admission avoidance, community alternatives, fidelity | scenario-controlled changes to mechanisms |
| **D17 Costs/outcomes** | 14 | workforce, ambulance, diagnostics, facilities, inpatient/community costs, implementation, patient time, utility, horizon, discounting, perspective | simulated resource quantity × unit cost |
| **D18 Initialisation/simulation/observation** | 20 | initial queues/resources/staff/inpatient state, residual times, warm-up, horizon, replications, RNG, CRN, event ordering, timestamp error, calibration/validation | explicit simulation-control and observation model |
