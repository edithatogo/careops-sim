| Type | What varies | Sampling level |
|---|---|---|
| Patient heterogeneity | attributes and latent patient characteristics | once per patient |
| Event stochasticity | a given patient's stochastic task/event outcomes | per eligible event |
| Staff/shift heterogeneity | persistent productivity/behaviour differences | staff member or shift |
| Parameter uncertainty | unknown fitted population parameters | outer-loop posterior/bootstrap draw |
| Structural uncertainty | alternative model mechanisms | separate model scenario |
| Measurement error | observed versus true timestamps/codes | observation layer |
