# Material modelling and publication risks

1. **Wrong denominator:** CCR 4.5% episode readmissions and CORE adult 4.0%
   patient readmissions must not be substituted for a local, unplanned 72-hour
   ICU-to-ward readmission probability.
2. **Ecological inference:** pooled ANZICS exit block is not the Cairns
   rate; the 1000-patient sample and intervention effects are illustrative.
3. **Double counting:** index discharge delay and subsequent ICU readmission
   are distinct episodes; marginal cost must exclude care otherwise required.
4. **Wrong financial interpretation:** released bed-days are not automatically
   realised cash savings, staffed capacity or QALYs; annual cost per available
   bed is not the avoidable cost of a discharged patient-day.
5. **Safety:** more appropriate readmissions may follow improved surveillance;
   mortality, deterioration, post-ICU care and ward burden are unmodelled.
6. **Simulation uncertainty:** triangular independent inputs are judgemental,
   not fitted joint posterior distributions, and there is no demand/bed flow.
7. **Security/rights:** public Git must not receive patient records, private
   operational data, proprietary costing, unpublished chats or workbook copies.
8. **Tooling/ownership:** upstream Conductor and Kairos pins remain unchanged;
   the CLI installer requires a separately authorised local environment.

Stop promotion on invalid source provenance, missing local cost validation,
current-head CI failures, undisclosed overlapping benefits or claims of
CHHHS-specific savings unsupported by observed data.
